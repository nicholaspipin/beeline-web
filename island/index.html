Design rules applied

  • The most important action or metric is always leading and stable; secondary facts never compete with it.
  • The billboard is a fixed right rail with a vertical separator. Pages move vertically and can never create blank space.
  • Normal progress uses Paper. Honey is reserved for LIVE and the immediate “NEXT” moment. Red means a problem the student must notice.
  • Expanded is a predictable enlargement of compact and wraps tightly around the TrueDepth camera.
  • Natural campus names are shortened before typography shrinks. No text uses ellipsis.
  • Alignment is optical first: icon mass, baselines, and perceived centers are tuned even when the raw boxes are not mathematically identical.
